Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Overview

This application (the "App") is a personal tool that uploads the user's own videos to the user's own TikTok account as drafts.

2. Data accessed

With the user's authorization, the App accesses only:

  • Basic TikTok profile information (display name and username), used to confirm the destination account.
  • Permission to upload videos to the account's drafts.

    • The App does not access followers, messages, comments, contacts, location or any other data.

    3. Data storage

    Authorization tokens are stored only on the user's own device. No data is stored on our servers, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

    4. Revoking access

    Users can revoke the App's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions, and by deleting the locally stored token.

    5. Changes

    This policy may be updated at any time. The current version is always available on this page.
